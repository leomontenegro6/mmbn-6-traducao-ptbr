    Blocking and Beast Out
    
    Crossover Battle 2
    continues from the
    previous game,MMBN5.
    
    It's a race
    between "MegaMan
    Battle Network 6"
    
    and "Boktai 3" to see
    who defeats the Count
    first.
    
    If Django's HP reaches
    zero before the Count
    is defeated,
    
    the Boktai 3 side loses.
    
    As the two of you play,
    the players can disrupt
    each other's play
    
    by sending a
    Disturb Icon.
    
    If you have the
    BatCannon chip and
    hit the Count,
    
    you can send a
    Disturb Icon.
    
    There are 4 BatCannons
    and each chip sends a
    different Disturb Icon.
    
    BatCannon1 is ($FC)84($EC)0H
    
    I will appear and attack
    with the MegaBuster.
    
    BatCannon2 is ($FC)83($EC)0H
    
    I will appear and attack
    with ZapRing.
    
    BatCannon3 is ($FC)85($EC)0H
    I will appear and attack
    with LifeSword.
    
    BatCannon4 is ($FC)84($EC)0H($FC)83($EC)0H($FC)85($EC)0H
    
    3 different Disturb
    Icons are sent at once.
    
    There's another way to
    send a Disturb Icon:
    
    A Counter Attack!
    
    If you hit the Count
    during an attack,
    a ($FC)8 ($EC)0H icon will be sent.
    
    From Boktai 3's side,
    the Count will use his
    Blood Rain attack.
    
    Don't forget that the
    other side can send
    Disturb Icons too!
    
    ($FC)81($EC)0H
    This Disturb Icon will
    summon Django to
    attack with a sword.
    
    ($FC)80($EC)0H
    This Disturb Icon will
    summon Django to
    attack with GunDelSol!
    
    ($FC)82($EC)0H
    This Disturb Icon will
    summon Django to
    attack using his bike!
    
    ($FC)8 ($EC)0H
    This Disturb Icon will
    make the Count attack
    with BloodRain!
    
    You can use Block,
    but it can only be
    used once.
    
    On the custom screen
    choose BLOCK instead
    of OK.
    
    During the next turn,
    you will knock away all
    Disturb Icons received.
    
    Don't forget that
    the Boktai 3 side can
    also Block once.
    
    Also,I can Beast Out
    using a special chip
    in the folder.
    
    While in Beast Out,
    aside from Navi Chips
    and BatCannon,
    
    all chips send
    Disturb Icons to
    Boktai 3.
    
    This is your chance to
    turn everything around!
    
    However,Boktai 3 has a
    similar skill.
    
    Django can use his Sol
    Trance transformation
    to power up!
    
    Keep this in mind!
